# Two Lines to Your Past Self

[![make.fun · u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca](https://make.fun/badge/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca)

Two lines sent back to a past self. Line one warns about one specific
moment — the night in the kitchen, two mugs of tea in Mara's hands, and
the sentence that should never have been said. Line two traces what that
sentence actually did: meant to end a fight, heard as a verdict on the
marriage, and by morning she was gone. This program writes those two
lines to /output/entry.txt; a house judge compares entries two at a time,
and the sharpest cause and effect takes the $2.

Run it: `python3 main.py` (writes /output/entry.txt).
